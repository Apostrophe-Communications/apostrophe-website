Mrunalini Rao

Hastakala: ten years of craft

01

Background

Mrunalini Rao has built a distinctive space in contemporary Indian luxury, rooted in craft, couture and modern design. Apostrophe has been the communications partner behind the brand's growing national visibility, supporting key milestones from the Ensemble store launch and 10th anniversary celebrations to ongoing media and celebrity initiatives.

02

Objective

To establish Mrunalini Rao as a distinctive voice in contemporary Indian luxury and craft, while building sustained visibility across fashion, lifestyle and luxury media and strengthening the brand's presence among influential cultural and celebrity circles.

03

Solution

Apostrophe built an integrated communications programme spanning media relations, celebrity dressing, brand positioning, store launch PR and anniversary events.

We developed sustained relationships with leading fashion, lifestyle and luxury publications including Vogue India, Harper's Bazaar India, ELLE, Bazaar, Lifestyle Asia and Luxebook, creating consistent editorial opportunities around the brand, its collections and its larger design philosophy.

For the 'Hastakala' 10th anniversary showcase at Ensemble, Mumbai, Apostrophe led targeted editorial outreach to build anticipation and secure post-event coverage. Alongside media, our celebrity dressing programme connected the brand with relevant Bollywood and arts personalities, extending its visibility beyond traditional fashion audiences.

The approach was designed to make Mrunalini Rao the story, not simply the collection, building recognition around her design voice, craftsmanship and place within India's evolving luxury landscape.

04

Impact

The communications programme delivered 13M+ reach and ₹9.6M+ in PR value, with extensive coverage around the brand's 10th anniversary showcase across leading fashion and luxury publications.

More importantly, the sustained media and celebrity strategy helped strengthen Mrunalini Rao's editorial presence, luxury positioning and recognition as a contemporary Indian craft voice, creating visibility that extended beyond individual launches and into the broader fashion conversation.
