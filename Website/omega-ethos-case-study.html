Omega × Ethos Watch Co.

An exclusive SIS activation, Ludhiana

01

Background

Omega, in association with Ethos Watch Co., brought its luxury watchmaking experience to Ludhiana through an exclusive SIS-led activation. The occasion called for a carefully curated experience that could engage the city's luxury consumers, watch enthusiasts and relevant media and influencer community.

02

Objective

To create and execute a seamless, premium launch experience that brought Omega closer to Ludhiana's luxury audience, while ensuring the right mix of media, influencers, watch enthusiasts and high-value guests.

03

Solution

Apostrophe served as PR & Event Partner, managing the event across guest curation, logistics and on-ground execution.

We handled venue identification, event setup, décor, menu selection and cost coordination, while curating and inviting relevant media, influencers and watch enthusiasts. A dedicated guest-lister was also brought on board to strengthen the quality and relevance of the audience.

From the initial planning through on-ground execution, Apostrophe managed the multiple moving parts of the experience to ensure a smooth, considered and brand-aligned event journey.

04

Impact

The activation created a focused platform for Omega and Ethos Watch Co. to engage Ludhiana's luxury and horology community, bringing together relevant audiences in an experience designed around the stature of the brand.

The seamless integration of PR, guest curation and event execution enabled Omega to extend its presence beyond the retail environment and create a more personal connection with its local luxury consumer base.
