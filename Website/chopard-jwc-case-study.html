Chopard × Johnson Watch Co.

L.U.C collection launch

01

Background

Chopard, in association with Johnson Watch Co., launched its L.U.C collection in New Delhi, bringing its exceptional watchmaking craftsmanship to an audience of discerning collectors, luxury consumers and horology enthusiasts. The launch required an experience that could communicate the sophistication of the collection while creating a genuinely engaging setting for the watch community.

02

Objective

To design and execute an immersive, luxury-led launch experience that brought together the right mix of media, influencers, collectors and watch enthusiasts, while creating a distinctive narrative around the craftsmanship and character of the L.U.C collection.

03

Solution

Apostrophe served as PR and Event Partner, managing the launch from concept through execution.

We led venue identification, event setup, logistics, décor, menu curation and guest invitations, bringing together a carefully selected audience of media, influencers, luxury consumers and watch enthusiasts. We also facilitated the collaboration with the J9 Watch Collectors community, creating a more authentic connection with the horology ecosystem.

To give the collection a more experiential narrative, we brought watch enthusiast and sommelier Magandeep Singh into the experience for a curated whiskey-pairing session. Each L.U.C timepiece was paired with a complementary malt, using the language of taste, craftsmanship and detail to bring the character of the watches to life.

04

Impact

The launch transformed a traditional collection showcase into a multi-sensory experience rooted in horology, craftsmanship and luxury culture.

The combination of a highly curated guest list, collector-community engagement and the whiskey-pairing experience created a more meaningful setting for the L.U.C collection, while strengthening Chopard and Johnson Watch Co.'s engagement with Delhi's luxury and watch enthusiast community.
