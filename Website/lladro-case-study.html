Lladró

India Uncovered at India Design

01

Background

Apostrophe partnered with Lladró around its participation at India Design, creating a multi-layered communications programme to introduce the brand's luxury design philosophy, artistic heritage and craftsmanship to India's influential design, architecture and lifestyle audiences.

02

Objective

To maximise Lladró's visibility around India Design, strengthen its credibility within the Indian design ecosystem, and demonstrate the versatility of its pieces through meaningful engagement with architects, interior designers, influencers and design-focused audiences.

03

Solution

Apostrophe built an integrated programme around India Design and AD associations, guest-listing, influencer engagement, architect collaborations and curated content creation, anchored by the Lladró India Uncovered campaign.

To generate pre-event anticipation, Apostrophe collaborated with influential design and lifestyle voices including Navneet Randhawa, Bahaar Rohatgi and Ridhi Khosla Jalan, using the India Uncovered narrative to create social media buzz ahead of the event.

The agency also facilitated collaborations with leading architects including Ajay Arya, Huzefa Rangwala, Jasem Pirani of MuseLAB and Karan Desai, strengthening Lladró's relationships within India's professional design community and reinforcing its credibility within the architecture and interiors ecosystem.

Three interior designers were further engaged to place Lladró products within homes across different aesthetics, demographics and locations, demonstrating the versatility of the pieces and how they could integrate into varied interior environments.

Apostrophe also managed guest-listing for the brand's India Design presence and facilitated influencer content generation at the booth, extending the physical showcase into digital conversations.

04

Impact

The programme created visibility for Lladró across design, architecture, interiors, influencer and lifestyle communities, while positioning the brand within the larger conversation around contemporary Indian design.

Through a combination of premium event associations, influential design voices, architect partnerships and real-world product placements, Apostrophe helped communicate Lladró's timeless craftsmanship, artistic legacy and versatility to a broader and highly relevant audience.
