Governor House

Two preview weekends

2Exclusive preview weekends
01

Background

Apostrophe partnered with Governor House to create visibility around the property's launch and establish it within the luxury hospitality and lifestyle ecosystem. The mandate brought together strategic PR, curated guest experiences and KOL engagement to introduce the property to a highly relevant audience.

02

Objective

To generate awareness and anticipation around the property through exclusive preview experiences, build relationships with influential media and tastemakers, and position Governor House as a distinctive luxury hospitality destination.

03

Solution

Apostrophe led the end-to-end PR and launch management across two exclusive pre-view weekends, combining media relations with carefully curated guest experiences.

A highly considered guest list brought together media, HNIs, influencers and tastemakers, including Geetika Sachdev, Pallavi Pasricha, Shubham Bhatnagar, Sayanti Halder, Angad Singh Gandhi, Rosella Stephen, Aishwarya Subramanyam, Ajay Nayyar, Jamal Shaikh, Sanskriti Sharma, Pawan Soni, Parth Bajaj, Puneet Yadav, Ruth D'Souza Prabhu, Aparna Sharma, Varun Rana, Anirban Blah, Kunal Walia, Kanu Sharma, Sam Kaur, Ruchika Mehta and Raman Dhingra.

The guest experience was designed around the property itself, with welcome lunches, curated dining experiences, wellness and cultural activities, traditional welcomes and immersive property experiences creating a considered introduction to Governor House.

On the PR side, Apostrophe managed guest outreach, coordination and RSVP management, facilitated media interactions and developed post-event storytelling across relevant luxury, lifestyle and hospitality platforms.

The programme was further supported through KOL and community interactions, extending the property's visibility through social and digital conversations beyond the preview weekends.

04

Impact

The two preview weekends created an immediate network of influential media, luxury audiences, KOLs and tastemakers around Governor House.

The combination of immersive hospitality, strategic guest curation and targeted media engagement helped establish the property within relevant luxury, lifestyle and hospitality conversations, while creating sustained opportunities for editorial and social amplification.
