Bally

SS24 Father’s Day preview

01

Background

Bally partnered with Apostrophe Communications to host a Father's Day experience at DLF Emporio, centred around the preview of its SS24 collection. The event was designed to bring the collection into an elevated, experiential setting while creating a meaningful occasion for Bally's luxury clientele and community.

02

Objective

To create an intimate, premium experience around the SS24 collection, strengthen Bally's engagement with its clientele, and generate meaningful brand visibility through a culturally relevant Father's Day moment.

03

Solution

Apostrophe led the event end-to-end, from guest-list curation and event ideation to partner management and on-ground execution. The experience brought together a carefully curated guest list within an elevated setting at DLF Emporio, complemented by bespoke food and beverage experiences through Caara.

Apostrophe also secured an alcohol partner for the event and introduced a live engraving experience, allowing guests to personalise their Bally selections and adding a distinctive layer of customisation to the occasion.

The event was further amplified through professionally produced visual content, extending the experience beyond the physical event and into Bally's digital ecosystem.

04

Impact

The experience created a distinctive and highly personalised expression of Bally's SS24 collection, combining product discovery, luxury hospitality and experiential customisation within a culturally relevant Father's Day occasion.

The initiative strengthened client engagement while creating content and visibility that extended the impact of the event beyond the guest experience.
